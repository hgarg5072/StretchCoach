# Stretch Coach Research Notes

Date: 2026-08-15

Public app patterns reviewed:

- pliability: guided mobility routines, daily sessions, body-area targeting, progress habits, and short routine durations.
- STRETCHIT: flexibility and mobility programs, all-level routines, goal-based recommendations, challenges, and video instruction.
- Seconds Interval Timer: large timer controls, voice cues for eyes-off training, custom interval structure, halfway alerts, next-up alerts, screen-on/background-friendly behavior, and no interruptions mid-workout.

Applied to Stretch Coach:

- Keep the routine editable and offline-first.
- Use real exercise imagery instead of abstract placeholders.
- Keep player controls visible on compact phones.
- Add voice modes, halfway cue, and next-stretch cue.
- Speak useful coach instructions from stretch notes during holds.
- Keep screen awake while a session is running.
- Show current stretch position and next stretch in the player.
